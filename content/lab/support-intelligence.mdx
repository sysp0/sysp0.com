Support conversations are full of answers to questions nobody asked yet: what breaks most, what takes longest, which replies actually help. As raw text they are hard to use. This pipeline turns them into structured data.

## How it works

Tickets and Jira conversations are pulled from SQL Server and modeled with dbt. LLMs then enrich them in layers: category, priority, resolution and agent quality. It runs every 30 minutes on Airflow.

## What it gave

Support conversations became structured data the team can analyze, instead of a pile of text.

## What I'm learning from it

Layering the enrichment keeps each step small enough to check on its own, and tracing every call makes it possible to see why the model answered the way it did.

**Stack:** LangChain, Langfuse, OpenRouter, dbt, Airflow, SQL Server.
