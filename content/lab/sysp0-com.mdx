This site is its own first experiment. The idea is simple: a personal site doesn't have to be a brochure. It can behave like the thing its owner builds.

## What it is today

- **URLs are concepts, not folders.** `/sys/me` is me, inside the system. There is no `/about` and no `/projects`.
- **The home page stays simple.** A short introduction and direct links to my engineering journey, experiments, and notes.
- **Persian comes first.** The default home page is Persian and right to left, with an English home at `/en`.
- **Content lives in files.** Every note and experiment is a file in the repo, checked at build time, so a broken entry fails the build instead of the page.

## Where it could go

These are ideas, not promises. They're here so I can see which ones I still want in a month.

- A small live demo for every experiment in this lab.
- A visible history of how the site itself changed, as a log of its own.
