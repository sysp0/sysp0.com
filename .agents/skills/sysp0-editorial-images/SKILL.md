# SYSP0 editorial images

Create purposeful images for SYSP0 articles. First identify the placement: article header/card or image inside the article. Read [the art direction](references/art-direction.md). Use the built-in image generation tool for raster illustrations. Use `assets/brand-master.webp` and the two `style-reference-*` images for palette, material, and restraint; do not copy a reference composition.

For article headers and cards (`image:` front matter and social preview), use sculptural geometric forms as the main subject, like the existing blog covers. Build a fresh composition around the article's core idea with deep navy shapes, warm ivory space, and a restrained orange accent. Do not use realistic hands, desks, laptops, documents, or other literal scenes as the header subject. Keep a clear focal point and a crop that reads at card size.

For images inside the article, make the specific process, evidence, comparison, or decision clear without relying on the caption. Use recognizable objects, human gestures, paper, tools, or workspaces when they explain the idea better than abstract geometry. Glass cubes, loops, and fine connecting lines are optional. Vary subject, crop, viewpoint, and silhouette between sections; do not repeat the header as an in-article image.

For both placements, keep the brand's ivory space, deep navy structure, restrained orange focus, tactile surfaces, and quiet editorial lighting. Avoid decorative shapes without a clear relationship. Keep images clear at their displayed size and free of text, labels, logos, and watermarks.

Save site-bound results under `public/blog/images/`, with descriptive names and optimized WebP variants when appropriate. Preserve user-provided diagrams as supplied unless asked to edit them. Include concise alt text that describes the visual idea, not decorative details.
